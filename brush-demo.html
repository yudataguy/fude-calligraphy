手習い · tenarai

Write Japanese with an ink brush — right in the browser

Drag on the paper with a mouse, finger, or stylus. The brush behaves like real 筆 — dwell to press the tuft open, sweep to ride the tip. How you lift decides the ending: stop dead and the stroke ends in a rounded 止め; flick off and it tapers to a dry 払い point. Corners swell like a pressed 折れ, and the stroke bulges to the brush-belly side the way kaisho does.

The brush writes the characters itself, in correct stroke order — medians from animCJK, per-stroke endings from KanjiVG. Up to 4 characters, laid out 縦書き. Needs the page served over http for the data files.
